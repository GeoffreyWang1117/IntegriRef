The L0-L4 Verification Stack

Each layer adds a deeper dimension of integrity analysis.

L0Foundation

Reference Existence Verification

Multi-registry cascade across 62 adapters. DOI/PMID/ISBN lookup, title fuzzy search fallback, field-by-field comparison (RapidFuzz + Jaccard), hallucination detection (336K refs/s), and retraction checking.

18,555 refs/s 62 registries Hallucination detect
L1Intent

Citation Intent Classification

SciBERT (110M params) fine-tuned on SciCite — classifies each citation as Supporting, Mentioning, or Contrasting. Heuristic fallback for CPU-only deployments with lexical cue matching.

86.0% F1 3-class CPU heuristic fallback
L2Semantic

Semantic Claim Verification

DeBERTa-v3-base fine-tuned on SciFact. Splits abstracts into sentences, scores each against the citing claim via NLI (entailment/contradiction/neutral). Exceeds human agreement.

93.5% accuracy > human 89.1% ONNX accelerated
L3Graph

Citation Graph Anomaly Detection

2-hop citation graph from OpenAlex. 7 anomaly detectors: orphan clusters, temporal anomalies, excessive self-citation, citation rings (CIDRE variant), Benford's law violations, reciprocal patterns, and citation bursts.

7 anomaly types 2-hop graphs Benford + CIDRE
L4Risk

Bayesian Integrity Risk Scoring

18 signals with literature-derived likelihood ratios. Domain-specific priors (cancer: 10%, CS: 2%). Four risk tiers: Low / Elevated / High / Critical. Confidence-weighted evidence accumulation.

18 signals 8 domain priors 4 risk tiers

Who Uses IntegriRef?

From individual researchers to major publishers.

Researchers

Pre-submission self-check: verify all your references exist, are correctly cited, and flag potential issues before peer review catches them.

Journal Publishers

Automated screening during submission: detect paper-mill products, sneaked references, citation manipulation, and fabricated citations at scale.

Funding Agencies

Grant application review: assess reference quality and integrity signals before committing research funds. Identify high-risk proposals early.

AI Platforms

LLM hallucination detection: verify citations generated by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI systems. The fastest-growing use case in 2025-2026.

University Libraries

Institutional integrity monitoring: track reference quality across departments, identify systematic issues, and support research integrity offices.

Investigative Journalists

Deep-dive analysis of suspicious publications: combine all five layers to build comprehensive integrity reports for retraction investigations.
